VIDAJARY app

IDEA
I want to build app for iphone. It should allow user to record short videos and merge them together. I dont want it to be video editor like imovies, i want it to be video recorder, like old VHS video cameras - you can turn on recording and it would automatically add the recorded video to the end of the video consisting of all videos recorded previously. It would have save (or export) button which would save the whole video to the library. 

The use cases for this is 
- you want to tape one video from different moments in your vacation, 
- you want to keep video diary, snapshots of few moments from each day
- you want to record short videos from your child / building the house / roadtrip, etc. but have it in form of one long video

So, you can have multiple projects open and based on the occasion you would add the video to the project. 

Original short videos can stay in library. 

User can add a video from library to the project, although the main flow would be to record the video in the app. 

When user open the app there should be the last selected project open with instant option to just record a new video

User should be able to preview the whole video before exporting.

No transitions, just plain cut.
